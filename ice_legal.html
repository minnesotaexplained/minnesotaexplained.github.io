COPAL Helpline

If you have been directly impacted by federal activity and need support, please call the COPAL Helpline at 612-255-3112.

Visit Website

MIRAC Deportation Defense

Contact miracmn@gmail.com to see if we are able to assist you or someone you know who is detained by Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).

Visit Website

Immigrant Law Center of Minnesota

If you are detained by ICE or calling on behalf of someone in the custody of ICE, call 651-641-1011 during their intake hours to receive assistance.

Visit Website

MN National Lawyers Guild Legal Hotline

Call 612-444-2654 for protest-related legal assistance, before, during or after an action.

Visit Website

Mid-Minnesota Legal Aid / Southern MN Legal Services

Free legal services for people with low income, older people, people with disabilities. Can assist with immigration, housing/tenant issues, family law, disability law, public benefits, and more. Call 1-877-696-6529.

Visit Website

Advocates for Human Rights

Free legal help to people in immigration detention, seeking asylum, and unaccompanied children. Serves all of Minnesota; must be at or below 200% federal poverty guidelines. Call 612-341-9845.

Visit Website

Adult Representation Services (ARS)

Serves Hennepin County residents only. Provides independent legal representation and support to people experiencing poverty in civil matters including immigration legal rep, housing/tenant defense, and child protection matters. Call 612-348-7012 or email contactars@hennepin.us.

Visit Website

Volunteer Lawyers Network Minnesota (VLN)

Free legal services/representation on a range of civil issues, including immigration and more. Call intake Monday through Thursday from 10am-1pm at 612-752-6677 or complete online intake form.

Visit Website

LawHelpMN.org

Self-help legal resource on a variety of legal issues, answers many commonly asked questions, provides forms/guides, and legal services search tool.

Visit Website

Delegation of Parental Authority (DOPA)

A legal document for parents making temporary arrangements to ensure your children are cared for by an adult if you are unable/unavailable.

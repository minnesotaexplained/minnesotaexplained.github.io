About Delegation of Parental Authority (this form)

If you are a parent who is concerned about being arrested, detained, or deported and separated from your children you should consider completing a Delegation of Parental Authority (DOPA). A DOPA allows you to designate another trusted adult to have the legal authority to care for your children in case you cannot. The DOPA can be notarized virtually, by City of Minneapolis Clerks, or in person at most UPS stores.

DOPA Duration

This DOPA gives your Attorney-in-Fact permission to:

• Get medical treatment for your child

• Enroll your child in school

• Provide a home, care, and supervision of your child

It does NOT give permission to: Consent to marriage or adoption of your child

Legal Requirement: By law, you must give or mail a copy of this document to any other parent within 30 days of signing it, unless:

• The other parent does not have parenting time rights or has supervised parenting time rights, OR

• There is an existing Order for Protection in effect against the other parent to protect you or your child

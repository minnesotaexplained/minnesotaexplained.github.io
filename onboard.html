🏢 What Are State Agencies?

State agencies are departments of Minnesota government that carry out specific functions. Each agency has a commissioner (like a CEO) appointed by the Governor, and together they employ thousands of Minnesotans to deliver services, from health and education to transportation and environmental protection.

The Legislature writes the laws and sets the budget. The agencies put those laws to work: they run programs, issue licenses, write detailed rules, and enforce them.

Did you know? Minnesota has over 20 major state agencies working every day on issues that affect your life.

🔍 Where to Find Agency Information

1. Official agency websites

Every state agency has its own website with detailed information about its programs and services.

  • Main portal: visit mn.gov to find links to all state agencies.
  • Agency URLs usually look like agency-name.state.mn.us or mn.gov/agency-name.
  • Example: Department of Health is at health.state.mn.us.

2. Minnesota State Directory

The Secretary of State publishes an annual directory of all state agencies, commissioners, and contact information.

💡 Pro tip: Search "Minnesota Blue Book" or "Minnesota State Agency Directory" to find the official list of agencies with their current leadership and phone numbers.

3. Public meetings

Many agencies post their meeting schedules and agendas online. Check individual agency websites for "Meetings" or "Calendar" sections.

📰 How to Stay Informed

  1. Subscribe to agency newsletters. Most agencies offer email updates on programs, rule changes, and public meetings. Look for "Subscribe" or "Email Updates."
  2. Follow on social media. Many agencies post real-time updates on social platforms.
  3. Check news and press releases. Find them under "News" or "Press Room."
  4. Review annual reports. Agencies publish yearly reports on their activities, accomplishments, and budgets.
  5. Monitor rulemaking. When agencies create new rules, they must hold public comment periods. See proposed rules in the State Register.

🗣️ Ways to Engage with Agencies

📞 Call or email

Every agency has a public information office. Contact details are on their websites.

📝 Submit comments

Agencies accept public comments on proposed rules and policies during official comment periods.

🎤 Attend meetings

Many agency meetings are open to the public. Check calendars for upcoming meetings.

📄 File complaints

If you have concerns about an agency's services, most have formal complaint processes.

📊 Finding Specific Information

Budget and spending

  • Minnesota Management & Budget (mn.gov/mmb): state budget information and agency spending.
  • Open Checkbook: search state expenditures and see where tax dollars go.

Employment data

  • Workforce reports: Minnesota Management & Budget publishes annual workforce statistics showing how many people work for each agency.

Performance and results

  • Agency performance reports: look for "Performance Dashboard" or "Results" sections on agency websites.

⚡ Quick Reference

Need help fast? Call 651-296-6013 (Minnesota State Operator) to be directed to the right agency or service.

Most commonly searched agencies

  • Driver's licenses & vehicle registration: Department of Public Safety, Driver & Vehicle Services
  • Taxes: Department of Revenue
  • Health insurance (Medical Assistance): Department of Human Services
  • Unemployment benefits: Department of Employment & Economic Development
  • Child care assistance: Department of Children, Youth & Families
  • Environmental concerns: Pollution Control Agency or Department of Natural Resources
Remember: State agencies work for you! They're required to be transparent about their activities and responsive to public questions. Don't hesitate to reach out if you need information or assistance.

About Electronic Signatures:

• This form allows you to add an electronic signature for your records

• The electronic signature is NOT legally binding for this document

• You MUST print and sign the generated PDF in front of a notary public with your photo ID

• Your Attorney-in-Fact does NOT need to sign in front of a notary, but must sign before using it

Parent/Guardian Information

Attorney-in-Fact Information

Note: "Attorney-in-Fact" is what the person you name is called - they do not have to be an attorney.

DOPA Duration

This DOPA gives your Attorney-in-Fact permission to:

• Get medical treatment for your child

• Enroll your child in school

• Provide a home, care, and supervision of your child

It does NOT give permission to: Consent to marriage or adoption of your child

Legal Requirement: By law, you must give or mail a copy of this document to any other parent within 30 days of signing it, unless:

• The other parent does not have parenting time rights or has supervised parenting time rights, OR

• There is an existing Order for Protection in effect against the other parent to protect you or your child

Electronic Signature

By typing your name, you are electronically signing this document. Note: You will still need to sign the printed document in front of a notary.
